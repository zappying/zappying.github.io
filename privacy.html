Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026 · Operator: DONGJIN PARK

zappying (currently at zappying.github.io, the "Service") is a collection of free web games run by DONGJIN PARK (the "Operator"). This policy explains what personal data we process, why, and how long we keep it.

You can play every game without signing in. By default, game progress is stored only on your device (browser storage) and is not sent to us. We process the personal data below only if you choose "Sign in with Google", submit a score to a leaderboard, or contact us.

We do not collect your real name (beyond what your Google profile shows), phone number, address or payment details. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

2. Retention

  • Account link and cloud save: kept until you delete them in Keep Going (Settings → Delete cloud data & account) or ask us to; deleted promptly on request.
  • Leaderboard nicknames and scores: kept while the leaderboard runs; deleted promptly on request.
  • Messages: kept for 1 year after we reply, then deleted.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law.

4. Service providers and international transfer

Sign-in and cloud saves run on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore) provided by Google LLC. Your data may be stored and processed in the United States or other countries where Google operates data centers. If you don't want this, simply don't sign in; your progress stays on your device and you can move it with a backup code.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data, and withdraw consent at any time:

  • Delete instantly in Keep Going: Settings → Delete cloud data & account
  • Or email us at the address below. We'll respond without undue delay after confirming it's you.

If you are in the EU/UK, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Children

The Service is a general-audience game site and is not directed at children under 13. Children under 13 (under 14 in Korea, or the age required in your country) should not sign in without a parent's or guardian's consent. If we learn that we hold such data without consent, we will delete it. Parents can request access or deletion using the contact below.

7. Device storage and cookies

Games save progress in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is removed if you clear site data. We show ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet (see How Google uses information from sites that use its services). You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ad Settings.

8. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Cloud saves can only be read or written by the signed-in owner (enforced by database security rules).
  • We keep the minimum data needed.
